About Chuck

I've been investing in stocks and options for nearly 20 years. In that time I've seen bull runs, crashes, long sideways stretches, and days when the market made no sense. I've made money and I've lost it, and every one of those markets taught me something. The biggest lesson is that steady, disciplined income usually beats chasing big wins.

All those years gave me the confidence to keep investing through every kind of market. It isn't the confidence of being right all the time. It comes from having a process I trust. I know how to size a position, when to collect premium, when to step aside, and how to keep one bad trade from turning into a bad year.

That's why I started The Earnings Wheel. Options have a reputation for being complicated and risky, and used carelessly they can be. Used with a plan, they can be a practical way for an everyday investor to earn income. Most of what's written about options is either too technical to follow or too hyped to trust. I want to close that gap.

Each letter walks through how I think about income strategies like cash-secured puts, covered calls, and the full wheel that ties them together. I explain the reasoning in plain English, show the math, and talk honestly about the risks and the trades that don't work out. When I hold a position I'm discussing, I'll say so.

You don't need a finance background to get value from this. That's my job. Your job is to learn the reasoning, ask good questions, and decide what fits your own goals and risk tolerance.

I'm not a financial advisor, and nothing here is personal advice. Thanks for reading. I'm glad you're here.

Chuck Thornton
