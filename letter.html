The Weekly Letter

One email a week, always free.

Each issue covers

  1. A note from me: what the market did and what I'm watching.
  2. Earnings on the radar: companies reporting soon, and why they're on my list.
  3. This week's ideas: two or three cash-secured puts or covered calls, with the strike, expiration, premium, breakeven, the case for the trade, and what could go wrong.
  4. My position: for every idea, I tell you whether I own it or have the trade on.
  5. Last week's scorecard: how every idea played out, good or bad.
  6. One lesson: a single concept explained in plain English.

It goes out every SEND DAY.
